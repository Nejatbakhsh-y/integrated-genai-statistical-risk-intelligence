# Integrated GenAI-Statistical Risk Intelligence

## From Pension Risk to Integrated Risk Intelligence

Evidence-grounded GenAI and statistical learning for temporally valid,
auditable, interconnected corporate risk early warning.

## Primary Research Question

Do evidence-grounded GenAI-derived interconnected-risk signals available at
the forecasting date improve out-of-sample prediction of subsequent corporate
defined-benefit pension funding deterioration beyond conventional structured
and conventional NLP information?

## Information Sets

X(i,t): structured pension, financial, market, and macro information.

Z(i,t): validated GenAI-derived interconnected-risk intelligence.

I(i,t): prespecified cross-risk interactions.

Primary comparison:

X
-> X + NLP
-> X + Z
-> X + Z + I

## Model Ladder

M0: Persistence

M1: Structured statistical

M2: Structured nonlinear

M3: Structured plus conventional NLP

M4: Structured plus GenAI risk vector

M5: Integrated X + Z + I

M6: Nonlinear integrated X + Z + I
